10 August 2022: Rapidus established by eight Japanese companies, total investment 7.3 billion yen.
February 2023: site selection announced - Chitose, Hokkaido; ~5 trillion yen fab investment.
September 2023: groundbreaking at Chitose. April 2024: METI up to JP 590bn additional support (total ~JP 1 trillion). FY2025: 802.5bn yen subsidies. April 2026: 631.5bn yen additional. 2nm mass production expected late 2027.
