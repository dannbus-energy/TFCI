Ember assessment (Oct 2025), based on auctions in Italy, Saudi Arabia, India and expert interviews: all-in BESS project capex outside China/US ~$125/kWh; core equipment ~$75/kWh + ~$50/kWh install. BNEF: core equipment fell 40% in 2024 to $165/kWh record low.
ESTIMATE BASIS: 2022 peak ~2.5-4x the 2025 level. 285 MWh x $400/kWh = $114M (range 85-171M). 2025 benchmark: 41 MWh x $125/kWh = $5.1M (expansion). 2020-era US: 1200 MWh x $350/kWh = $420M (range 300-500M).
