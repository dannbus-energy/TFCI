Equinor's first hybrid power complex starts operations (December 8, 2025). Equinor and its Brazilian subsidiary Rio Energy have started commercial power production at the Serra da Babilonia Solar facility in Brazil. First hybrid asset in Equinor's power portfolio: 140 MW solar + 223 MW wind capacity. Accumulated annual production ~236 GWh (143,000 Brazilian households). Energy sold by Danske Commodities. Rio Energy acquired 2023 with operating 223 MW wind complex and project pipeline.
