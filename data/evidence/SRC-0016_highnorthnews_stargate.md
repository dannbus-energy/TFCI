Aker Nscale plans a data center for AI in Korgen, Nordland. Aker and Nscale, together with OpenAI, is investing in an AI Gigafactory at Kvandal outside Narvik under the name Stargate Norway. In mid-September [2025], an agreement worth approximately NOK 60 billion was entered into with Microsoft in connection with this establishment. Aker finalized its investment in Nscale and the Narvik joint venture, creating Aker Nscale.
