Supported by EUR 619 million ($712 million) in funding from the German federal government and the state of Lower Saxony, the project will bring 200 MW of electrolyser capacity online in 2026, with the remaining 100 MW scheduled for completion in 2027. FID in 2024 after RWE successfully operated a 14 MW pilot plant. Annual output up to 49,000 tonnes; 30,000 t/yr to TotalEnergies Leuna from 2030 (15-year agreement).
