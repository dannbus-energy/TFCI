First turbine begins operations at 960-MW He Dreiht (Nov 26, 2025). The Eur2.4 billion ($2.8 billion) project was awarded in a 2017 tender without subsidies. Construction started in spring 2024. 27 of 64 turbines installed. Start of commercial operations planned for early 2026, full COD middle/end-Q2 2026. Consortium of Allianz Capital Partners, AIP and Norges Bank IM holds 49.9%. Initial end-2025 target slipped to spring 2026.
