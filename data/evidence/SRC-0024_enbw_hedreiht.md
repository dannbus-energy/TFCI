EnBW He Dreiht: first wind turbine generated the first kilowatt-hour of electricity yesterday evening (release Nov 26, 2025 -> first power Nov 25, 2025) and fed it into the grid. 27 of 64 wind turbines built, all set to be commissioned by summer 2026. Total output 960 MW, Germany's largest offshore wind farm. First use of the 15 MW Vestas turbine. Built ~85 km northwest of Borkum WITHOUT state funding. Consortium (Allianz Capital Partners, AIP, Norges Bank IM) owns 49.9%. Around 2.4 billion euros are being invested.
