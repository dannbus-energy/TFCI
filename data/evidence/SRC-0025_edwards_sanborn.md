Edwards & Sanborn Solar and Battery Storage (Kern County, California; Terra-Gen developer, Mortenson EPC): largest integrated solar-plus-storage facility in the United States, full operational status early 2024. 875 MWdc solar, ~3,300 MWh BESS, 1,300 MW interconnection. Construction begun December 2020. Over 1.9 million solar modules (First Solar). Off-takers include Starbucks, Clean Power Alliance, PG&E, San Jose. Total cost estimated at over US$2 billion.
