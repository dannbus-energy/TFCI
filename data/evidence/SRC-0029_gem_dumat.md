Dumat Al Jandal wind farm is an operating wind farm in Dumat Al Jandal, Al Jouf, Saudi Arabia. Status: Operating. Commissioning year: 2021. Nameplate capacity: 400 MW. Type: Onshore. Owner: EDF Renewables (51%); Masdar Clean Energy (49%). Operator: Masdar.
