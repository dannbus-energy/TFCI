On June 29, 2018, Vistra Energy announced that it will develop a 300 MW / 1,200 MWh energy storage system at Moss Landing, connecting to the 500 kV grid; expected commercial operation by end of 2020. Construction began December 2019; Phase 1 began operating at the end of 2020. Phase 2 (100 MW / 400 MWh) completed August 2021 (total 400 MW / 1,600 MWh). Phase 3 (350 MW / 1,400 MWh) commissioned August 2023 (total 750 MW / 3,000 MWh). Expansion to 1,500 MW / 6,000 MWh approved August 2020, not built.
January 16, 2025: fire destroyed the Phase 1 building (300 MW / 1,200 MWh); ~80% of that battery section burned; 1,200 residents evacuated. Vistra wrote off $400 million on the damage. Cleanup began late 2025; Monterey County restricted battery projects after the incident.
