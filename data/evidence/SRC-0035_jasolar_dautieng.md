Tay Ninh, Vietnam: JA Solar is powering the world's largest semi-submerged solar project - the 500MW Dau Tieng Solar Power Plant in Tay Ninh Province, Vietnam. By building on water, this project has been operating reliably for over six years, saving 464 hectares of farmland while generating clean, sustainable energy.
Aggregate investment in the Dau Tieng 1 and Dau Tieng 2 projects should amount to around 9,100 billion VND (around US$397.6 million).
