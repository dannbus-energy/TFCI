Since 2018, Xuan Cau Holdings has pioneered renewable energy development in the province through the Dau Tieng 1, 2 and 3 Solar Power Plant Complex, which has a combined capacity of 500 MW, making it the largest solar power complex in Southeast Asia at the time. The complex was developed across 720 hectares, representing only 2.6 per cent of the total surface area of the Dau Tieng Reservoir, and was completed in approximately 10 months.
