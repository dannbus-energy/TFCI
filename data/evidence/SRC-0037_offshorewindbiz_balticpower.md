Poland Receives Its First Electricity from Offshore Wind as Baltic Power Starts Delivering (July 10, 2026). Electricity generated by the Baltic Power offshore wind farm has entered Poland's national power grid, marking the first time offshore wind electricity is added to the country's energy mix. 54 of 76 turbines installed, with the first now generating. Offshore work began at the start of 2025. Construction completion scheduled for this autumn. 76 turbines x 15 MW = ~1.2 GW; ~4 TWh/yr; ~3% of Poland's electricity demand; 1.5+ million households. Project of ORLEN and Northland Power, ~23 km offshore near Choczewo and Leba, 130 km2 area. ORLEN total investment programme PLN 380bn (group-wide, NOT project capex).
