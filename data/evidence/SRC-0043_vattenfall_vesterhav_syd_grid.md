Vattenfall completed 100 percent of Vesterhav Syd. First power was delivered November 10, 2023; all turbines were connected to the grid by January 9, 2024. Facts: Vesterhav Syd 168 MW and Vesterhav Nord 176 MW, total installed capacity 344 MW. Vattenfall won the Vesterhav Syd tender in 2016.
