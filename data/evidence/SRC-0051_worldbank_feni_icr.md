Implementation Completion and Results Report for Bangladesh Scaling-up Renewable Energy Project P161869. Component 1, Feni Utility-Scale Solar PV: capacity increased from 50 MW to 75 MW; electricity supplied to the grid from February 19, 2024; COD reached April 1, 2024. Estimated cost at completion: USD 75.7 million; original component cost USD 89.17 million.
Ledger: USD 0.0757bn estimated capex at completion; commissioning 2024-04-01. The earlier appraisal budget is retained as context only.
