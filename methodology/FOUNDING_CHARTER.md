# TFCI Founding Charter (v0.1, 2026-09-28)

The Transition Finance Credibility Index measures whether transition-finance
commitments become delivered reality. It is a decision input, not a decision:
it does not equate investment volume with human benefit, and it does not
instruct capital allocation.

## Mission boundary
TFCI measures delivery credibility. Human survivability is the reason to
build it; survivability itself is broader than deployment and is NOT scored
by this index.

## Non-negotiable evidence rules
1. Exact-claim match: evidence counts only for the specific company, project,
   site, date, and amount claimed.
2. Assertion-level provenance: every claim traces to its origin, not merely
   to a URL count.
3. Evidence is archived and hashed where legally retainable; where it is not,
   the limitation is recorded, not hidden.
4. Quotes, amounts, and dates are machine-checked against archived content
   before they count.
5. Missing evidence is labeled "not verified under protocol X" - never "false".

## Publication rules
Observed facts, analyst judgments, model suggestions, missing data, and
uncertainty are visually and structurally distinct on every profile.

## Institutional independence
Funding disclosure, recusals, public methodology versions, and an external
challenge procedure with recorded dispositions. Commercial work never
influences benchmark decisions.

## Revision protocol
Nothing is edited; everything is appended and versioned. Methodology changes
ship as new versions with disclosed back-casts. Published historical scores
are never overwritten.

## Outcome accountability
The index pre-registers what its scores are expected to predict and publishes
annual calibration reports including its misses.
